1
Grab Your Latest Backup

Take your latest .db backup (from your USB drive, Google Drive folder, or downloaded snapshot) and your media files.

2
Run Restore Wizard on Any PC

On the new computer, open the Stocks Library folder, double-click restore_backup.bat, and select your backup file.

3
Launch Relay Server

Start start_relay.bat. All studio accounts, quotas, and downloads are 100% restored in under 30 seconds!
